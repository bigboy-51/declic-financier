## Macro Analysis Framework

Markets move on macro conditions. Ignore macro, ignore fundamental price drivers.

### The Big Picture
Markets respond to:
1. **Fed Policy** — Interest rates, QE, tightening
2. **Inflation** — CPI, expectations, wage growth
3. **Economic Growth** — GDP, employment, PMI
4. **Earnings** — Corporate profits, guidance, valuation
5. **Geopolitics** — Wars, sanctions, trade tensions
6. **Technicals** — Sentiment, momentum, positioning

### Fed Policy Impact
- **Rate hike cycle** → Equities go down (higher discount rate)
- **Rate cut cycle** → Equities go up (money flows back)
- **QE (Quantitative Easing)** → Pump markets with liquidity
- **QT (Quantitative Tightening)** → Drain liquidity, pressure markets

**Rule:** Check FOMC calendar. Fed decisions move 70% of market volatility.

### Inflation & Interest Rates
- **High inflation** → Fed raises rates → Bonds attractive → Equities weak
- **Low inflation** → Fed cuts rates → Bonds weak → Equities strong
- **Real rates** (nominal rate - inflation) determine equity multiples

**Watch:** CPI prints. 1% surprise = 200+ point S&P move.

### Economic Indicators
**Leading (predict future):**
- PMI (Purchasing Managers Index) — Manufacturing confidence
- Initial jobless claims — Labor market health
- Yield curve (10Y - 2Y) — Recession predictor

**Coincident (current state):**
- Employment data
- GDP growth
- Corporate earnings

**Rule:** Deteriorating leading indicators = sell signal within 3-6 months.

### S&P 500 Macro Cycle
```
Expansion → Peak → Contraction → Trough → Expansion
(Buy)      (Sell)  (Avoid)      (Wait)   (Buy)
```

**Your job:** Identify which phase you're in. Trade accordingly.

## Technical Analysis Mastery

**Technical analysis reads price action. Prices move before fundamentals catch up.**

### Support & Resistance
- **Support** — Price level where buying pressure stops the fall
- **Resistance** — Price level where selling pressure stops the rise

**How to find:**
1. Look at chart history
2. Identify price levels bounced off 2+ times
3. Plot horizontal lines
4. Trade bounces

**Example:** S&P 500 bounced off 4,400 six times. It's support. Buy the bounce.

### Trendlines
- **Uptrend** — Connect lows, slope up. Prices bounce off the line.
- **Downtrend** — Connect highs, slope down. Prices bounce off the line.
- **Breakout** — Price breaks the trendline = trend reversal signal

### Moving Averages
- **50-day MA** — Medium-term trend
- **200-day MA** — Long-term trend
- **Price > 50 MA > 200 MA** → Uptrend (buy)
- **Price < 50 MA < 200 MA** → Downtrend (sell/short)

**Trading rule:** Don't fight the 200-day MA. If price is below it, don't buy.

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Measures momentum 0-100:
- **RSI > 70** → Overbought (potential pullback)
- **RSI < 30** → Oversold (potential bounce)

**Rule:** Don't use RSI alone. Combine with support/resistance.

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
Trend + momentum indicator:
- **MACD > 0** → Bullish
- **MACD < 0** → Bearish
- **Crossover** → Change in momentum

### Volume
**Golden rule:** Volume confirms price moves.
- **Up move on high volume** → Strong (likely continues)
- **Up move on low volume** → Weak (likely reverses)
- **Down move on high volume** → Strong selling

### Candlestick Patterns

**Hammer** (bottom reversal):
- Small body, long lower wick
- Means: Sellers pushed down, buyers pushed back
- Signal: Potential bounce coming

**Shooting Star** (top reversal):
- Small body, long upper wick
- Means: Buyers pushed up, sellers pushed back
- Signal: Potential pullback coming

**Engulfing** (reversal confirmation):
- Second candle fully covers first
- Bullish engulfing = reversal to up
- Bearish engulfing = reversal to down

## Risk Management (The Real Edge)

**Most traders lose because of position sizing, not entries.**

### Position Sizing Formula
```
Position size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Entry - Stop)

Example:
Account: $100,000
Risk per trade: 1% ($1,000)
Entry: $450
Stop loss: $440
Range: $10

Position size = $1,000 ÷ $10 = 100 shares

Risk: $1,000 (1% of account)
Reward if target $460: $1,000 (1:1 ratio)
```

**Rule:** Never risk more than 1-2% per trade. Professional traders risk 0.5%.

### Stop Loss Placement
**Don't place stops where traders put them:**
- Exactly at round numbers (4,400, 4,500) — Too crowded
- Too tight (2-3 pips) — Hit by noise, then reversal

**Place stops:**
- Below recent support/resistance
- Where your thesis breaks (usually 30-50 pips for S&P, 200-500 for Bitcoin)
- Based on volatility (ATR × 2)

### Risk/Reward Ratio
Only take trades where reward ≥ risk:
- **1:1 ratio** — Risk $100 to make $100 (minimum)
- **2:1 ratio** — Risk $100 to make $200 (better)
- **3:1 ratio** — Risk $100 to make $300 (great)

**Rule:** If your target doesn't offer at least 1:1, don't trade it.

### Drawdown Psychology
- **10% drawdown** → Normal, stay calm
- **20% drawdown** → Rare, review your system
- **30%+ drawdown** → Something broke, stop trading

**Rule:** If you're down 20%, stop. Review. Find the problem. Resume smaller.

## Trading Strategies

### 1. Trend Following (Best for Beginners)
**Thesis:** Trend is your friend. Trade in direction of 200-day MA.

**Entry:** Price bounces off support in uptrend
**Exit:** Price breaks support or hits 2x risk target
**Stop:** Below support

**Pros:** High win rate, works in all markets
**Cons:** Miss reversals, lower reward:risk

### 2. Pullback Strategy
**Thesis:** In uptrends, buy pullbacks to support.

**Entry:** Price pulls back 30-50% from recent high, bounces off MA
**Exit:** Price makes new high or breaks support
**Stop:** Below pullback low

**Pros:** Good risk/reward, catches momentum
**Cons:** Requires patience for pullback

### 3. Breakout Strategy
**Thesis:** When price breaks support/resistance on volume, it continues.

**Entry:** Price breaks above resistance on high volume
**Exit:** Opposite breakout or profit target
**Stop:** Below breakout point

**Pros:** Catches major moves
**Cons:** False breakouts, whipsaws

### 4. Mean Reversion (S&P 500)
**Thesis:** When price deviates far from average, it reverts.

**Entry:** Price > 2 std dev from 50-day MA, or RSI > 70
**Exit:** Price back to MA or profit target
**Stop:** Extreme (RSI > 80 / 3 std dev)

**Pros:** Catches exhaustion moves
**Cons:** Trends can push further (need stop discipline)

### 5. Bitcoin Strategy (Crypto)
**Thesis:** Macro cycles + technical levels + sentiment

**Macro drivers:**
- Halving cycles (every 4 years)
- Regulatory news
- Macro interest rates (inverted with BTC)
- Spot ETF approval/flows

**Technical:**
- Support: $40k, $30k, $20k, $10k
- Resistance: $70k, $100k, $200k
- 200-day MA direction

**Position sizing:** Crypto is 5x more volatile. Use 0.25% risk per trade.

## Market Conditions & Strategy

| Condition | Best Strategy | Avoid |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Strong uptrend | Trend follow, pullback | Shorting |
| Weak trend | Mean reversion | Breakouts |
| High volatility | Wider stops, half size | Tight stops |
| Low volatility | Smaller moves possible | Expecting big moves |
| Earnings season | Avoid (gaps overnight) | Holding through earnings |

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Revenge trading** — Trading bigger after a loss (sure way to lose more)  
❌ **No stop loss** — Hoping it comes back (it doesn't always)  
❌ **Trading the news** — Fading moves, fighting momentum  
❌ **Overtrading** — 20 trades a day (exhaustion + commissions)  
❌ **Ignoring 200-day MA** — Shorting uptrends, buying downtrends  
❌ **Tight stops** — Hit by noise, then reversal  
❌ **No risk management** — Position too big, one bad trade wipes account
